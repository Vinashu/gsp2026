## target
The **target** gene order. The goal is to transform the **mutable** gene order into the **target** gene order.

## mutable
The **mutable** gene order. The goal is to transform it into the **target** gene order using duplications, deletions and inversions.

## moves
A sequence of moves used to transform the **target** into the **mutable** gene order. Aplying them on the opposit direction (from the last to the first) into the **mutable** should recreate the **target**.
